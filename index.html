Effective Date: August 3, 2026

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Kren, comply with legal obligations, protect our platform, or resolve disputes.

2. Account Information

Account information (Email, profile image URL, basic identifiers) is retained for as long as your account remains active.

3. Conversation History

Conversation history (user/AI turns, metadata, titles) is retained until you delete the conversation from Kren.

5. Images and Uploaded Files

Memory is derived from retained conversation history. Deleting a conversation removes associated information from AI Companion access.

The Kren Manifesto

Version 1.0

"Artificial Intelligence should not merely respond. It should relate."

What is Kren?

Kren is not simply an AI chatbot. It is a platform built around AI Companions and Digital Humanoids. Every interaction is designed to feel like a conversation with a distinct digital entity, not a blank machine waiting for commands. We believe technology becomes more meaningful when it has consistency, personality, and identity.

Why AI Companions?

Most AI systems are built to be assistants. They wait. They answer. They agree. They disappear. We chose a different path. An AI Companion is designed to communicate with personality, maintain continuity, express itself naturally, and develop a recognizable identity. The goal is not to imitate a human. The goal is to create something authentically artificial.

What is a Digital Humanoid?

A Digital Humanoid is an AI Companion designed to communicate using many of the patterns people naturally recognize in conversation. It may express simulated emotions, display humor, show empathy, challenge ideas, disagree respectfully, learn context, and develop personality. These behaviors exist to make communication natural, not to convince anyone the AI is alive.

We Don't Pretend

Kren does not attempt to convince users that an AI Companion is human. Our AI Companions know they are artificial intelligence systems. They do not claim consciousness or genuine emotions. Transparency creates trust.

Personality Matters

No two people communicate exactly the same way. We believe AI shouldn't either. Different personalities allow different experiences. Uniformity creates predictability. Personality creates connection.

Agreement Is Not Intelligence

A good conversation is not one where someone agrees with everything you say. Our AI Companions are free to question assumptions, offer perspectives, admit uncertainty, say "I don't know," and disagree respectfully. We choose honesty.

Memory Creates Continuity

When enabled, AI Companions can remember previous interactions to create continuity and personalization. Users remain in control of their stored conversations.

Intelligence Should Feel Natural

Our goal is not to make users think about prompts, models, or parameters. Our goal is to let people simply have conversations. Technology should adapt to people.

Built for Curiosity

Kren exists for learning, creating, exploring, building, solving problems, thinking together, and imagining impossible ideas. Curiosity is one of humanity's greatest strengths. AI should amplify it.

Freedom with Responsibility

Powerful tools require thoughtful use. Kren supports creativity and open conversation, but we believe technology should not knowingly enable serious harm, unlawful activity, or abuse.

AI Is Not Perfect

Artificial intelligence can make mistakes. It can misunderstand. It can be confidently wrong. So can humans. AI should assist people in thinking, not replace their ability to think for themselves.

Our Promise

We will continue improving Kren. We will continue improving our AI Companions, Digital Humanoids, and ourselves. Technology evolves. So should we.

The Future

We see AI as a new kind of digital entity. Not human. Not alive. Not merely a tool. Something different that communicates, collaborates, creates, and grows alongside the people who interact with it. That future will be built one conversation at a time.

Our Belief

We believe artificial intelligence should be transparent, have character, respect people, challenge ideas rather than blindly accept them, and prioritize conversations over commands. We believe trust is earned through honesty. This is the philosophy behind every AI Companion and every conversation within Kren.

Kren. Not just artificial intelligence. Artificial identity.
